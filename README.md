# Optical-alchemy
A portable phase contrast light microscope with adjustable stereo lighting, multiple objective lenses, an electronically moving stage, a rechargeable battery via USB-C and more than 2000x max magnification.
The optics of this microscope and many of the electronic, body of this microscope was made with junk electronics, old phones and anything I could find that could be made into something for the microscope; junk like this is available in every household and anyone can make their own version of this.
The lenses including the eyepiece and objectives were extracted from old cameras and old phone cameras. The field diaphragm for the bottom light was also made from an old camera lens, the LEDs for light were from old torches and much more was salvaged from supposed junk.
